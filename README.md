One voicemail greeting as if a cat recorded it — at most 120 characters. Mittens is napping on your keyboard, invites you to meow after the beep, and denies knocking your mug off the table… before sheepishly confessing she did. Pure cat: keyboards as beds, gravity experiments with mugs, zero remorse.

> Hi, it's Mittens. I'm napping on your keyboard, so meow after the beep. No, I didn't knock your mug off. ...Okay, I did.

Run it: `python3 main.py` — writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`
